# Ateljé — brand and product context

This glossary names the brand concepts that connect the atelier's clothing, petite patterns and craft details. It describes the creative world, not the website implementation or commerce system.

## Brand principles

**Proportion**:
The relationship between a garment's dimensions, placement and silhouette, considered in relation to the person wearing it. Petite describes a proportion context, not simply a smaller size.
_Avoid_: Reduced size, scaled-down body

**Connection**:
The shared design language that makes garments, patterns and adornments part of one considered whole.
_Avoid_: Separate business lines

## Offerings

**WEAR**:
Reconstructed clothing, including contemporary garments inspired by Korean hanbok forms and Scandinavian restraint.
_Avoid_: Costume, historical replica

**MAKE**:
Sewing patterns developed with petite proportions in mind from the first pattern line.
_Avoid_: Standard pattern made smaller

**ADORN**:
Hand-tied Korean knots, tassels, jewellery and small details designed to complement the garments.
_Avoid_: Unconnected accessories shop

**Look**:
A considered combination of garments and adornments. It expresses the brand's design relationship; it is not itself a product.
_Avoid_: Outfit bundle

**Reconstructed garment**:
A finished piece made by reworking an existing garment or textile into a new silhouette.
_Avoid_: Upcycled as a generic product label without material context

**Demo piece**:
Fictional editorial content used to demonstrate a future product presentation. It is not for sale and must be replaced or clearly retained as sample content.
_Avoid_: Live product
